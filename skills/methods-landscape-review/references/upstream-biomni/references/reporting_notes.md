# Infographic Notes

Use the `GenerateImage` tool unless otherwise specified to create a conceptual or schematic
summary grounded in the verified artifacts. Do not fabricate numeric values; any number shown
must come from `performance_claims` or `evidence_table`.

For a methods comparison, summarize the options, their source-supported tradeoffs, and the
conditions that affect method selection. For a topic review, summarize what is established,
contested, and open. Keep the content legible and scientifically qualified.

After generation, run a `Read` `media_output_check` on the image and regenerate it if text is
garbled, content is crowded, or unsupported claims or numbers appear.
