# Step 2 — preliminary epitope evidence

Prepared 2026-09-21. Candidate regions remain unselected.

The prepared structures provide two experimentally observed antibody-contact
regions that are candidates for the requested distinct epitopes:

| Candidate | Structural source | Canonical contact region (preliminary) | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 20G5 Fab, RCSB 9LY5/9LY6 | approximately 177–188 and 214–230 | Reproducible 20G5 contact region in the IgC domain; 9LY6 supplies a second Fab copy of the same antibody context, not a second epitope. |
| B | T3CL11 nanobody, RCSB 9LME | approximately 64–89 and 123–130 in the resolved partial target after affinity-tag removal and explicit model-to-canonical anchoring | Independent structural contact region in the N-terminal IgV domain; partial structure and nanobody geometry limit direct transfer to a Fab. |

These are evidence-backed candidate regions, not a final epitope selection.
Their separation is based on residue mapping and domain location; it does not
prove that a 1A + 1B IgG can engage both sites simultaneously on one native
4Ig molecule. The approach vectors, glycans, membrane clearance, Fab reach,
and possible epitope accessibility on living cells still require structural
testing.

The next scientific decision is whether to carry forward the 20G5-like region
as candidate A and the T3CL11-like region as candidate B for the preliminary
same-antigen Fab-pair geometry check. No antibody sequence or epitope anchor
has been generated from this report.
