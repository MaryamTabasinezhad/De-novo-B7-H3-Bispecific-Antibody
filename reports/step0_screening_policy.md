# Step 0 — provisional screening and developability policy

Date: 2026-09-21. This is a planning recommendation for the approved complete
1A + 1B human IgG1-like antibody baseline. It does not authorize target
preparation, sequence generation, software installation, or compute.

## Proposed staged policy

Keep binding, same-molecule cis geometry, internalization, Fc activity, and
developability as separate evidence tracks. Do not combine correlated predictors
into one composite score or allow a favorable affinity prediction to rescue a
severe assembly or developability concern.

Retain sequence and structure diversity through each filter. The workflow's
provisional parent-panel scale is approximately 20–50 candidates per epitope
when the design pool supports it; this remains configurable rather than a hard
user-selected budget. Preserve moderate-scoring geometrically distinct parents
to reduce model-selection bias.

Use three risk dispositions: hard exclusion for a defined severe incompatibility;
review flag for a plausible but uncertain liability; and context-dependent risk
when the outcome depends on the assembled antibody, formulation, or assay.
Record the reason for every exclusion and retain borderline candidates when they
add independent information.

## Required evidence tracks

For each A/B parent and complete construct, record sequence liabilities,
humanness/immunogenicity hypotheses, folding and interface confidence,
self-association/aggregation proxies, chemical-stability liabilities, Fab/Fc and
membrane clashes, pairing purity risk, cis reachability, internalization
hypothesis, and Fc-receptor access. Computational values remain hypotheses.

For advancement, require no unexplained severe assembly or folding concern,
interpretable same-antigen geometry, and a visible risk register. Stability,
aggregation, internalization, Fc-mediated killing, and product purity require
purified-protein or cell-based measurements; computational triage cannot declare
them demonstrated.

## Open policy items

The numerical candidate budget, exact hard-exclusion thresholds, formulation
conditions, Fc activity assay, internalization assay, and experimental promotion
cutoffs remain unresolved. These should be configured before production-scale
design, with provisional values labeled and revisited after a pilot.
