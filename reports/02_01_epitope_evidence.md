## User-confirmed preliminary design pair

| Arm | Design target | Why it was carried forward |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **8H9-like exposed IgV1 FG-loop region**, centered on approximately canonical 126–129 (`IRDF`) | 8H9 provides experimentally mapped IgV evidence, and the independent coordinate screen confirms exposure of R127 and F129 in the membrane-distal IgV1 neighborhood. |
| **B** | **Coordinate-derived exposed IgC1 patch 2**, approximately canonical Q228, Q229, H232, S234, T236, T238, Q240 and R241 | This is a broad exposed IgC1 surface identified from the 9LY6/unbound-model screen and is spatially separated from the IgV1 target. It is a design hypothesis adjacent to, and partly overlapping the edge of, the broader 20G5 region; it is not assumed to be identical to the 20G5 footprint. |

The short rationale is to combine a membrane-distal IgV1 surface with a
separate IgC1 surface that is accessible in the structure. The 8H9 evidence
supports real B7-H3 binding for Arm A, while the coordinate-derived IgC1 patch
provides a distinct surface hypothesis for Arm B rather than simply copying a
known antibody contact map.

This pair is a preliminary design decision, not proof that a 1A + 1B IgG can
engage both sites simultaneously on one native, glycosylated 4Ig molecule. The
approach vectors, glycans, membrane clearance, Fab reach, competition with
20G5/8H9, and living-cell accessibility require the preliminary same-antigen
Fab-pair geometry check before sequence design. No antibody sequence, linker,
or final paratope has been generated from this report.
