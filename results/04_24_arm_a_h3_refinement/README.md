# 04_24 — Arm A H3 contact recovery

The [per-loop table](04_24_loop_contacts.tsv) compares the archived 421
and 412 backbones on intact canonical 4Ig. In 421, heavy-chain H3 residue
103 backbone N is 3.197 Å from the B7-H3 I126 carbonyl O. H3 is the only
loop within 4.5 Å of I126 in 421. In 412, the closest pair to I126 is
also in H3, at 5.508 Å (H101 carbonyl O to I126 carbonyl O). The
distance exceeds the contact threshold by 1.008 Å. This supports
testing a local H3 change. It does not identify a favorable contact
between those two oxygens. H3 already supports contacts to residues
127 and 129 in 412, so rescore all three after sampling. H2 supports
the residue 128 contact.

The loop table and refinement input were generated by
`tools/04_design_sequence/04_24_arm_a_loop_contacts.py` using the tracked
04_22 seed and placement PDBs and the canonical AFDB v6 target. The
[HLT input](04_24_seed_412_input.pdb) keeps the seed 412 coordinates
after crop Cα alignment and maps chain T to canonical residues 29–169.
